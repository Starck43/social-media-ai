has no general service-account/token management contract. Existing shared billing
P2-T08 is deferred, and Q-12 already owns pre-public email confirmation/recovery.

cross-tenant operations fail closed; existing user sessions and operator boundary
remain compatible; audit/export/privacy requirements are explicit. No new
infrastructure or legal-compliance claim is authorized by listing these options.
**Dependency/risk:** P11-T08/08/10 as selected; commercial scope stays with P2-T08,
privacy/activation with their release gates, not a duplicate roadmap here.

## Owner decision and retained identity boundary

**Decision recorded 2026-10-27:** retain current capabilities; do not select or
authorize a new enterprise requirement now ([owner choice](https://app.notion.com/chat?t=40636385470c802da6c300a99fd79436)).
This satisfies the card's explicit retain-current-capabilities outcome; it is
not a mandate for a broad enterprise build.

The documented identity boundary remains unchanged:

- Platform/account roles remain distinct from workspace membership roles, and
  workspace authority remains scoped to the active bound user and membership
  ([TENANCY](../TENANCY.md#roles-and-permissions),
  [ACCESS_MIGRATION](../ACCESS_MIGRATION.md)).
- Client API access remains bearer-JWT and caller-membership scoped; tenant
  selectors do not widen the caller's workspace authority
  ([TENANCY](../TENANCY.md#bypass)).
- No service-account/token-management contract, SSO/OIDC/SAML, SCIM,
  organization-level identity policy, or data-residency requirement is selected
  or authorized by this decision. No compliance or infrastructure claim follows
  from the enterprise label.

Reconsider only after an explicit owner selection of a named customer/use case
and its trust model, credential/session lifecycle and revocation/rotation,
role mapping, tenant boundary, retention/legal boundary, and acceptance matrix.
Any later implementation must use the future acceptance criteria above.
