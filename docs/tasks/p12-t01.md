# P12-T01 — Domain-grouped `/app` navigation and `ready=False` section scaffolding

Registry: [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md#p12); current status/owner/dialogue live there.
Parent: [P12](../phases/p12-domain-workspace-expansion.md).

## Scope and acceptance

Extend `NavItem` in `app/web/nav.py` and the sidebar template `app/web/templates/web/_shell_nav.html` to organize `/app` navigation into five domain groups (`Overview & Agent`, `Monitoring & Analysis`, `Knowledge & Documents`, `Communication & Follow-up`, `Cases & System`) without breaking existing URLs, `section_of()`, `allows()`, or the 4-item `MOBILE_NAV_KEYS` bar.

Register upcoming expansion sections (`connectors`, `knowledge`, `documents`, `communications`, `followups`, `cases`) with `ready=False` and their target `required_permission` keys so unauthorized users do not see gated sections and authorized users see disabled `· скоро` entries until each page lands.

Accept with offline unit and template-rendering checks covering permission filtering, active-section marking, `ready=False` rendering, and mobile nav bar preservation.

Unassigned. Independent UI/navigation foundation for P12-T02 through P12-T06.
