### Scope and corrections

The existing `knowledge_documents` / `document_revisions` store is extended by
0142 with business types, tags, contact/case-evidence/owner references, a current
revision pointer, author/change/decision attribution, and tenant-owned templates.
API CRUD and HTML management use the same permission-checked transactional
service. Body or material metadata edits append an immutable revision and
invalidate the prior decision. Stored `draft_invalidated` remains compatible and
is projected as draft plus an invalidation flag. Unsafe downgrade refuses before
DDL rather than losing new-only data.

Independent migration/model review and service/API/HTML review inspected the
original source. Two validation defects were corrected in separate commits
`16ffaae35478262176faea4d0837371052982e17` and
`144da5956b1939831a14dd7dc604f75ed63556a6`:

- Metadata-only edits of valid legacy revisions over 200,000 characters were
  rejected by the new-creation body cap. Stored bodies now retain compatibility;
  explicitly supplied replacement bodies retain the cap and NUL validation.
- Nested NUL strings/keys in template schemas reached PostgreSQL JSONB instead of
  producing validation failure. The contract rejects them before persistence,
  preserving the JSON size/finite-value check before traversal and accepting
  ordinary Unicode and literal escaped text.

Corrections affect `app/services/knowledge/document_contracts.py` and
`tests/knowledge/test_p19_t01_service_unit.py`. An independent reviewer returned
PASS for this exact corrected source; final test results are recorded below.
Migration/model blobs are unchanged between the initial and corrected sources.
