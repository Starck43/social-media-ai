(`Service-plan finalization refused: unresolved sponsorship or missing backfill evidence`)

`q14_compare_schemas` then reported `DRIFT` for exactly two causes, neither introduced by
this change; both were corrected on the owner's instruction (2026-11-15):

- `tenants.plan_holder_user_id` is `NOT NULL` in `public` (`0155` finalized it) and nullable

  `dormant_service_plans` (`not settings.USER_SERVICE_PLANS_ENABLED`) and tolerates exactly

  `allowed_differences`. Any other column difference, and the same split with the switch
  on, still reports drift. Covered by
  `tests/tooling/test_q14_compare_dormant_nullable.py` (**4 passed**).
- `public.contact_channel_stats` lacked the three latency columns and the two
  `..._latency_..._nonneg` checks from `0144`. `public` applied `0144` while it was still
  provisional (`0cb69afd`); the final DDL landed in place later (`8f81d229`, which also
  re-placed the revision) without a new revision id, so the already-applied revision never
  re-ran. Repaired on `public` with the same `alembic.Operations` calls as the revision
  (`latency_total_ms` and `latency_sample_count` `bigint NOT NULL server_default 0`,
  `latency_pending_outbound_at` `timestamptz NULL`, plus both check constraints) inside one
