## Goal

Remind users about deadlines through their preferred channel.

## Baseline

- Notifications service and preferences (`app/services/notifications/`); quiet hours (P12-T04).

## Plan

1. Model `planner_reminders` (task, remind at, channel, state, sent at, snoozed until); presets 1 day/1 week/1 month and custom times.
2. Runtime job claims due reminders, respects quiet hours through the existing implementation, and delivers through `app/services/notifications/` — the only notification owner (D18). The channel list is what `messenger.py` implements (D19); a configured-but-unimplemented channel needs the sender capability from [P15-T06](../audit/p15-t06.md), and the card must say what happens meanwhile instead of dropping silently.
3. Snooze (1 h/1 day/1 week) and reschedule actions from the notification and UI.
4. Optional AI reminder suggestions as non-binding proposals.
