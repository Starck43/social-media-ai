## What actually makes that path red today

Not the coverage flag. It is the shared `test_schema` being ahead of this checkout: the calendar
branches created `calendar_choices`, `calendar_events` and `source_objects`, and `test_schema` is
stamped at a revision that also lacks `planner_reminders` and `notification_deliveries`. Any run
that bootstraps on it aborts before the first test, so the recorded invocation cannot be replayed
against `test_schema` at all — with or without `--cov-config=/dev/null`.

Two separate, already-registered defects explain the remainder:

- the literal `test_schema` guard that rejected the isolated probe — [P11-T33](../../archive/tasks/p11-t33.md);
- the `0145_planner_event_planning.downgrade()` that drops `planner_reminders` and
  `notification_deliveries` created by `0143`, reached from an independent run (P19-T08). That is
  a migration defect and needs its own card; it is reported to the owner rather than fixed here.

## Limits of this closure

- The closing evidence is the absence of a reproduction across four invocation shapes on two

  `sys.modules` keys between aborting and passing runs) is still the right next step.
- Probe schema `p11t32_probe` was dropped after the run and verified absent.
