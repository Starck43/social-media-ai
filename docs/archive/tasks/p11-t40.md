| --- | --- | --- |

The failing case is pure inventory (`importlib.metadata.distributions()`), so the

suites need; the two profiles have to stay separate, which is exactly what
`.ci/README.md` already says. Nothing in the repository has to change.

## Remaining red is a different defect (P11-T46)

After `0157_p21t15_atomic_admission` landed, the manifest exits 1 in **both**
profiles on three `tests/tooling/test_p11_t24_schema_guards.py` cases, not one:

```
test_the_bulk_table_map_covers_every_bulk_guarded_table
test_frozen_baseline_and_post_baseline_migrations_match_guard_module
test_frozen_baseline_and_later_migration_function_bodies_match_guard_module
```

The cause is the P11-T24 static extractor, not the environment: `0157` creates
its four guards through a module helper (`_immutable(table, trigger_stem,
message)` with `f"{trigger_stem}_immutable"` and a derived `function` name), and
the extractor drops helper-built triggers as "run time only", so the module's
`llm_token_allowance_reservations`/`llm_dispatch_reservation_events` guards
appear "module only". Registered as [P11-T46](p11-t46.md); the P11-T24 contract
belongs to that card, not to this tool-profile task.
