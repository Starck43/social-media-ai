## Finding

The card waited on a separate `audio` access permission. That gate was incomplete: a permission
cannot create a method. Checked on 2026-11-11:

- VK disabled the public audio API on **2016-12-16**; audio-section methods became unavailable
  except uploads (VK roadmap note).
- The official method index `https://dev.vk.com/ru/method` lists no Audio or Music section. The
  nearest neighbour is `Podcasts.searchPodcast`, which is a different category and was never
  requested.
- The `audio` access right **is** still listed and can be granted to an app by individual approval
  (`https://dev.vk.com/ru/api/privacy`), but no documented public **search** method consumes it.
- Unofficial audio-token clients exist and are out of scope: the project uses official methods only.

Nothing arrives if VK support says yes, so `deferred` overstated the remaining work.

## Accepted change

- registry: `music` → `unsupported`, reason `no_promised_public_search_method`, no
  `official_method`, no `adapter_operation`; the gate raises before dispatch even if a record is
  forged to `enabled`;
- `VKCollectionAdapter.search_audios` and `_audio` remain as a token-free contract mapper that
  nothing dispatches (recorded as such in its docstring);
- dropped the now-unused `REASON_PERMISSION_EVIDENCE` constant;
- restated the category tests and the `docs/COLLECTION.md` paragraph, which also still described
  video/apps/market as `deferred` after they had shipped `mocked`.

## What does not change for a subscriber

Music was unavailable as `deferred` and is unavailable as `unsupported`. Only the status label and
reason become truthful; no category becomes usable. Reopening requires VK to publish a search
method, which would be a new card rather than a resumed one.
