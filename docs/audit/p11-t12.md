## Scope of this slice

The card allowed a contract definition before any persistence. This PR delivers
that contract and nothing executable:

- `docs/ACTION_AUDIT.md` — the envelope (event/tenant/occurred-at/actor/action/
  resource/outcome/reason/request/job/receipt), the three separate outcome paths,
  the access and export rules, the allowlist redaction policy, the retention
  position, and the verification list a future implementation must satisfy.
- `docs/MODELS.md` — one paragraph stating that the cross-surface action audit is
  a proposal, not a shipped model or store.

No model, table, migration, hook, route, exporter or retention job is included.
The diff against `dev` is exactly those two files (134 added lines, 3 changed).

## Acceptance

- **Denied/success/rollback/unknown distinction — met at contract level.** The
  document routes `succeeded` through the business transaction, `denied` and
  `rolled_back` through a distinct safe logging path, and `unknown` through its
  own category with an explicit no-automatic-retry rule and a statement that a
  correlation ID is not a replay key. It forbids collapsing the three into one
  stream or one field.
- **Tenant-filtered reads and export — met at contract level.** Every query
  starts from the authorized workspace boundary; finding a row by event ID before
  filtering is explicitly forbidden; support access is read-only; cross-workspace
  access is not implied. Views and exports are bounded by time window and page
  size.
- **Retention and immutable-event permissions — met at contract level.** The
  contract is application-level append-only and says plainly that it is not
  database-level tamper-proofing. Rows are classified as the existing PRD-06
  `action-audit` data class; retention must be finite and owner-approved before
  enforcement, and no numeric period is invented because the source policy has
  none. Deletion, legal holds and cleanup verification stay separately
  authorized.
- **Reuse of existing identities — met.** `bot_actions.history`, jobs,
  entitlement usage, delivery and billing/LMM records stay authoritative; the
  audit stores `receipt_ref` references and explicitly refuses to become a second
  money or delivery ledger. An audit row or log is never a payment receipt.
- **No secrets or raw arguments — met.** An allowlist, not a denylist. Raw tool
  arguments, prompts, customer content, provider payloads, SQL, exception text
  and credentials are excluded, including from denied/rolled-back logging.
