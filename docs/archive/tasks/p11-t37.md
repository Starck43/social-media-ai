## Context and defects

Four issues were identified while bootstrapping the local development and `test_schema` environment at revision `0155`. After the rebase onto `dev` `0a5c2ea3` (P21-T13, PR #488 merged) two of them are already delivered upstream and left this lane:

1. **`test_schema` guard omission for `0154_p2t17_service_plan_storage`** — delivered by P21-T13 (`10d9b0a2`) as `service_plan_policy_immutable` plus its four triggers in `scripts/schema_guards.py`. Dropped here to avoid duplicating the objects the merge would otherwise carry twice.
2. **AST parser failure in `tests/tooling/test_p11_t24_schema_guards.py`** — delivered by P21-T13 (`10d9b0a2` tuple-target loops and `==`/`!=` comparison support, `16c927728` for the regression test, whose docstring records that one parser copy owns both lanes). `dev`'s parser is a superset; `tests/tooling/test_p11_t24_schema_guards.py` passes unmodified at `0a5c2ea3` (`23 passed`).
3. **Stale factory double in `tests/security/test_web_session_cookie_security.py`**: `app/main.py:create_application` calls `openapi_options(settings.ENVIRONMENT, settings.API_DOCS_ENABLED)`, iterates `BUSINESS_TAGS.values()`, and registers `AdminCSRFMiddleware`. The AST-extracted harness lacked `API_DOCS_ENABLED`, `openapi_options`, `BUSINESS_TAGS` and `AdminCSRFMiddleware`, so every method failed with `NameError: name 'openapi_options' is not defined` (`12 failed, 24 passed` at `0a5c2ea3`). Retained in this lane.
4. **PostgreSQL 15 CIDR netmask and dormant `0155` seed in `scripts/migrations/q14_validate_baseline.py` (and `q14_rehearse_bridge.py`)**: `SELECT inet_server_addr()::text` returns `'127.0.0.1/32'` (or `'::1/128'`) on TCP loopback in PostgreSQL 15, so `address not in (None, "127.0.0.1", "::1")` refused the authorized local target and passed a CIDR string to `PGHOST`. Separately, after `command.upgrade(config, "head")` on a disposable `test_schema`, `tenants.plan_holder_user_id` is `NOT NULL`, whereas dormant ORM metadata (`Tenant.__table__.c.plan_holder_user_id.nullable == True`) and `_seed_reference_rows()` require the dormant nullable column when `USER_SERVICE_PLANS_ENABLED` is `False`. Retained in this lane.
