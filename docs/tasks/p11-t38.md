# P11-T38 — CI secret-candidate drift triage

Registry: [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md); parent: [P11](../phases/p11-structure.md)
(accepted phase, this is a registered follow-up). No prerequisites.

## Goal

Bring the `scripts/ci/checks.py` secret gate back to a state where a new finding
means "look at this now": every candidate has been triaged into a real secret
(removed/rotated) or a justified baseline entry, and the gate stops reporting
known noise on untouched files.

## Why this exists

The gate compares scanned candidates against `.ci/secret-baseline.json` and fails
on anything not already baselined. A drift report on untouched files was first
noted on 2026-11-13 in local scratch (`.agents/tmp/state.json`, never portable
evidence) and reproduced on 2026-11-16.

**Measured 2026-11-16** (hash-only run of the check on `dev` `6d7305fb`; the
script prints path/type/hash, never the matched text):

- 374 candidates found, 287 in the baseline, 94 outside it.
- Samples inside the drift: `app/api/v1/endpoints/onec.py` and
  `app/services/calendar/service.py` (`Secret Keyword`), several
  `docs/audit/**` and `docs/archive/tasks/**` records (`Basic Auth Credentials`,
  `Hex High Entropy String`), `scripts/verify_p17_t05_migration.py`,
  `scripts/verify_p21_t10_prompt_safety.py`,
  `local_connector/packaging/linux_formats.py`,
  `tests/actions/test_p13_t04_unit.py`.

The drift is not automatically noise: two of the samples are application code.
Nothing here may be dismissed without looking.

## Scope

- Re-run the check and produce the full hash-only candidate list for the current
  `origin/dev` head.
- Classify each new candidate: (a) a real credential or key, (b) a fixture or
  documentation example that can never be a live credential, (c) a scanner
  false positive that deserves a filter/allowlist entry rather than a baseline
  record.
- Only (b) and (c) may be resolved by touching the baseline or the scanner. A
  baseline refresh with no per-entry reason is not acceptable: it silences the
  only signal that a secret was committed.
- Category (a) is an owner decision, not executor work: it needs rotation, and
  rotation/publishing changes credentials. Raise it under
  [owner questions](../process/COLLABORATION.md#owner-questions) with the paths
  (never the values) and stop that part.
- Keep evidence hash-only. Do not paste matched text, context or line numbers into
  the card, PR or commit message.
- Out of scope: changing scanner filters to hide a category wholesale, enabling
  detect-secrets verification (it performs external requests and stays disabled
  in this lane), and any history rewrite.

## Steps

1. Reproduce the drift on a fresh `origin/dev` and record the head SHA, the
   candidate count and the baseline count.
2. Group the new candidates by path family and classification.
3. For (b)/(c): write the baseline/scanner change with one line of justification
   per family, and rerun the gate to show it returns clean.
4. For (a): open the owner question, list the paths, and leave the entry
   unbaselined so the gate keeps failing until it is resolved.
5. Record the final "clean gate" evidence and the unrun pieces in the card.

## Acceptance

- `scripts/ci/checks.py` reports no new secret candidate on the accepted head, or
  the only remaining failures are the escalated real-secret entries with their
  owner question linked.
- Every baseline entry added by this task names its reason; no blanket refresh.
- Evidence is hash-only and reproducible with the recorded command.

## Rollback and boundaries

Baseline edits revert with the commit. A real secret cannot be "un-committed" by
this task; rotation is the owner's action and stays outside this card.
