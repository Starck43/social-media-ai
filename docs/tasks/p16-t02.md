## Plan

2. Runtime job claims due reminders, respects quiet hours through the existing implementation, and delivers through `app/services/notifications/` — the only notification owner (D18). 3. Snooze (1 h/1 day/1 week) and reschedule actions from the notification and UI.
4. Optional AI reminder suggestions as non-binding proposals.
