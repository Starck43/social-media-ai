## Scope and acceptance

Let a registered user select and purchase a service plan for a workspace and
activate the resulting plan entitlement after a verified payment event. Keep
subscription checkout and service-plan state separate from personal LLM payer
consent, balances, usage settlement and provider tariffs. Select the payment
provider and define its sandbox contract before implementation. Sandbox checkout
is in scope; live production purchase and activation require a separate
owner-authorized rollout gate and must not be implied by sandbox PASS.

Accept when registration/workspace selection, plan comparison, sandbox checkout,
verified activation, cancellation, expiry, renewal and downgrade have explicit
states and owner-visible receipts; webhook/callback authenticity, replay,
idempotency, duplicate/out-of-order events and retry behavior are tested; failed
or unknown payment never grants entitlement; downgrade/revocation is enforced on
queued and future tasks; and workspace/payer/plan rights cannot cross tenants.
Test with provider mocks or sandbox credentials only. P2-T09 remains the
production verification gate for real LLM/provider usage billing; it does not
certify subscription purchase. Q-12 is relevant only if the selected public
launch flow requires email recovery; do not make recovery a general prerequisite.

## Ownership and dependencies

Depends on the entitlement model and enforcement contract in P2-T15. Does not
implement corporate/shared payer accounts in P2-T08. Does not grant VK API scopes
or replace per-user VK authorization. Paid plan/team limits continue to honor the
deployment-wide `MAX_WORKSPACE_MEMBERS` ceiling.
