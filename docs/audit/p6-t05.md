## Finding and fix (`f3a3f3eb` + `5112dbec`)

`SourceAdmin` and `PlatformAdmin` inherited the generic SQLAdmin raw delete
path, which bypasses the per-workspace source-admission lock and
active-task/last-source refusal in
`app/services/collection/source_deletion.py`; a platform delete would also
cascade its sources. The accepted remedy (task-card option *disallow unsafe
raw administrator deletion*) is class-local: both views set `can_delete =
False` and override `delete_model` with an unconditional 403 before any row
lookup, covering single, bulk, foreign and malformed requests for every
identity including privileged callers. Workspace web/API/CLI deletion
policy, admission locks, schema, FK cascades and entitlements are
unchanged; `docs/MODEL_DELETION.md` corrected to the disabled-administrators
contract. The audit added the missing real-storage acceptance
`tests/admin/test_p6_t05_source_delete_db_guards.py` (`5112dbec`).
