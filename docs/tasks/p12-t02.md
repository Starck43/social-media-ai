## Scope and acceptance

Provide a dedicated `/app/connectors` management surface unifying authorized social, web/video/mail research sources, and optional paired local connectors under explicit capability declarations:
- Display per-connector object/operation capabilities (separating read/sync from outbound send/delete), credential owner, payer binding, and incremental sync watermarks.
- Surface redacted health/reauth/revocation diagnostics without exposing raw secrets or provider exceptions.
- Enforce explicit coverage and modality disclaimers (e.g., transcript analysis is not full audiovisual analysis; paired local connectors expose only selected canonical paths and types with cloud-transfer visibility and device revocation).

Accept with route/permission tests, redacted-error rendering checks, and mocked connector capability/revocation fixtures.
