Context gates are server-side in `app/web/chat.py`: 403 on tenant mismatch, on a
forged `context_type`/`context_id`/`boundary` against the session selection, on a
`PermissionError` from `resolve_context`, and for any boundary other than `cloud`
(including `local_only`) — refused before `handle_web_message`, with a test
asserting zero turns. Cards and the panel render only through `x-text` and bound
`:href`; remaining `innerHTML` assignments in `chat.js` are static SVG/icons and
markdown goes through the `safeMarkdown` allow-list.

## Limits

client-side fallback — currently safe (`x-text` only) but a second source of truth
that can drift.
