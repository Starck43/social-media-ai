## Findings

No blocking findings. Points checked and cleared:

1. **Public-import contract.** Every name the refactor moved out of
   `base_manager.py`, `job_manager.py` and `agent_task_manager.py` (50 symbols,
   including `LookupCompiler`, `Prefetch`, `prefetch`, `claim_next`, `mark_done`,
   `reap_stale`, `parse_date`, `build_dates_payload`, `validate_cron`) is still
   reachable through the base classes and mixins. Symbol-level scan of every
   `from app.models.managers.* import ...` statement in `app/` resolves on the
   branch. `_calculate_content_stats`, `_make_json_serializable`, `_num`,
   `extract_cost_info` and `per_1k` likewise survive in the AI seam modules.
2. **MRO shadowing.** `JobManager` and `JobLifecycleMixin` share no method name;
   neither do `AgentTaskManager` and `TaskInputsMixin`. Extraction cannot silently
   prefer a mixin body over a local override.
3. **Scope boundaries.** `app/web/`, `app/admin/`, `app/celery/`,
   `app/core/database.py` and every Alembic migration are untouched, as the card
   and the P11-T11/P11-T12 ownership notes require.
4. **Test integrity.** No new `skip`/`xfail`. Two assertion lines are replaced by
   stricter forms — a source-order assertion (`await outer.commit()` must precede
   the recorded commit) and an explicit `attempts=3` state update instead of an
   equality check on a stale `replace(...)` value. Nothing was removed to obtain
   green.
