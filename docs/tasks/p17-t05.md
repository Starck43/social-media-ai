## Goal

Control who may query which 1C data and who may approve writes.

## Baseline

- Workspace roles and permissions ([TENANCY](../platform/TENANCY.md#roles-and-permissions)).

## Plan

1. Permissions `onec.query`, `onec.export`, `onec.command.propose`, `onec.command.approve`; default: owner/admin all, manager query+propose, analyst query, viewer none.
2. Per-connection entity allowlist by role.
3. Audit view filtered by connection/user/operation; export of the audit view itself audited.
