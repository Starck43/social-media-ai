## Deliverable

One sidebar a manager can read in a glance: three pinned daily entries (home, chat, tasks), then domain groups ordered by
how often the work is touched, with no section that promises a page that cannot deliver its own name.

Final structure:

| Zone | Entries |
| --- | --- |
| pinned, no heading | Сегодня · Задачи |
| Агент | Чат с агентом · Аналитика · Рассылки · Источники · Сценарии |
| Общение | Переписка · Контакты |
| Документация | Документы и версии · База знаний · Файлы · Закупки |

Decisions encoded in the structure:

1. **Pinned block has no heading** — a group title above three everyday entries adds noise. A divider separates it from the
   first group heading; it stays visible when the sidebar is collapsed (headings hide, entries stay).
2. **No section without substance.** «Договорённости» and «Одобрения» lose their slots: the first has no writer and
   becomes a dated task ([P22-T15](p22-t15.md)), the second becomes a decision on the task card ([P22-T08](p22-t08.md)).
   Their routes, logic and pending counter stay (see the retirement tables in those cards and in [P22-T12](p22-t12.md)).
3. **One group per subject, not per type of page.** «Агент» is a single story in reading order — conversation, what it
   produced (Аналитика, Рассылки), what it reads from (Источники, Сценарии). Separate «Отчёты»/«Ассистент» groups split the
   same subject and left one of them with a single item. «Общение» holds Переписка and Контакты; «Сообщения» would
   overpromise content the metadata-only registry does not store.
4. **Names say what the page holds.** The digests surface is the history of what went out, its cost and a manual send, so it
   is «Рассылки» rather than a singular «Рассылка»; frequency and recipients stay in Настройки → Каналы доставки.
5. **Operator surfaces leave the daily sidebar.** The 1C connection pages stay behind Настройки → Мои подключения, and the
   job queue moves to Настройки → Пространство → Диагностика behind the `job.view` permission.
6. **Setup entries are not navigation.** `settings` stays a start-page option, not a menu item.

## Behaviour

- Group headings render in source order, only when the label changes; a `pinned` item carries an empty label so it never
  emits a heading.
- `section_of()` resolves the deepest matching href, so `/app/knowledge/files` owns «Файлы» rather than falling back to
  «База знаний».
- The pending-approval counter renders on «Задачи» (`data-nav-approvals-badge`) and the header «На согласовании» chip
  keeps its own link to `/app/commands`.
- The mobile bar keeps its four keys: home, chat, correspondence, tasks.
- Permission filtering is unchanged: `allows()` hides a section the actor may not open instead of rendering a dead link.

## Out of scope

- Role-specific nav variants. Visibility is permission-based only; a manager/operator split is a separate decision.
- Renaming routes, redirecting legacy URLs, deleting the retired pages — [P22-T12](p22-t12.md).
- Page headings of the retired surfaces (`/app/actions` still says «Предложения действий»): they leave the sidebar, so their
  titles are cosmetic until the redirect lands.
- Task card contents, «Ждут моего решения» and the commitments filter — P22-T08 and P22-T15.

## Acceptance

- The rendered desktop and mobile sidebars match the table above, in order, for an actor with all permissions, and omit
  exactly the sections the actor cannot open for a restricted actor.
- No heading is duplicated by its first item, no group has a single item, and the agent group reads conversation →
  results → inputs.
- Collapsed sidebar shows the pinned entries without headings; the divider separates the pinned block from the first group.
- The approvals badge appears on «Задачи» when a pending item exists and disappears when it does not.
- «Диагностика» appears in the workspace settings tab only with `job.view`.

## Rollback

The change touches navigation data and one template condition. Reverting the branch restores the previous sections and the
badge on the approvals item; no migration, no data write and no route removal is involved.
