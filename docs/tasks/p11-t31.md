## Problem

`pytest tests/notifications/ -q` on `dev` at `adf3160a` is red: **24 failed, 5 errors, 447 passed**
(6:58). The suite was written before the tenant-admission, workspace-snapshot and digest-contract
changes that later landed, so it no longer describes the shipped system.

Two representative failures, from the auditor's run:

- `test_digest_e2e.py` (all five cases, collection-time errors):
  `app.core.tenant_context.TenantContextError: Source admission requires the owning workspace`,
  raised from `app/models/managers/job_manager.py:316` — the fixture creates a job without the
  owning workspace in context.
- `test_web_notifications.py::test_notification_read_one_marks_single`:
  `resp.json()["ok"] is False` at line 118 — the handler now refuses the call the old test expected
  to succeed.

Full failing set: `test_digest_send_current_boundaries.py` (1 failed + 1 subfailure),
`test_digest_send_postgres_acceptance.py` (1), `test_merged_notifications_acceptance.py` (7),
`test_notification_delivery.py` (10), `test_web_notifications.py` (2), `test_digest_e2e.py`
(5 errors).

Without this, no notification-related change can be accepted on a green baseline, and a genuine
regression in the same files hides among the noise.

## Plan

1. Triage each failing test in two buckets: fixture stale (repair the fixture, keep the assertion
   intent) and real regression (fix the product or, if the intended contract changed by owner
   decision, restate the assertion and cite the decision).
2. Repair the same underlying causes in one place each: tenant/workspace context in the job and
   digest fixtures, role/membership snapshot helpers, delivery-failure payload scrubbing, and the
   `ok` envelope of the read/read-all endpoints.
3. No product change without evidence: every product edit carries the failing assertion it restores.
