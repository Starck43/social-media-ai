## Persistence and safety

Alembic **0121**, parent **0120**, adds two internal tables, a unique account
identity and constrained states. Connection + secret writes are atomic.
Fresh actor/context, active user/workspace/web membership, assigned role and
caller ownership are checked on every operation. Mutations/checks require the
fixed `source.create` workspace grant. Auth locks and connection row locks
serialize check/edit/disable/revocation, including a cancelled check waiting for
its socket worker. Disable erases the local secret even without an encryption
key; remote app-password revocation remains the user's responsibility.
Downgrade drops only the two new tables and their connection data; secure backup
is required. Legacy personal secrets and source records are unchanged.
