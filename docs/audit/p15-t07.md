## Delivered

- `app/services/communications/recommend.py` — deterministic ranking over
  currently authorized routes; preference dominates activity, activity dominates
  volume, ties are stable. Only consented **and** reachable channels are eligible;
  no channel yields `channel: null`, confidence `0` and a reason.
- `recommend_channel` rechecks tenant, active actor, live role from storage,
  `contact`/`unifiedmessage` VIEW rights in both grants and permissions, a
  non-merged contact, and a real sender route through the accepted
  `resolve_route` consent/route contract. Email is the only route with evidenced
  sender state today; identifiers alone never imply reachability (D15).
- No sending, no provider call, no LLM hint. Ranking is advisory and states that
  delivery authorization is rechecked at send time.
- `GET /api/v1/communication/contacts/{id}/recommend-channel` with
  `403`/`404`/`400` fail-closed responses and no leaked internals.
- Incremental channel statistics in `ingest.py`: first in-order inbound after the
  latest observed outbound per channel, one sample per outbound; ties and late
  arrivals count toward frequency but never as latency evidence.
- Migration `0144_contact_channel_latency.py` (additive; `down_revision = "0142"`).
- Unmerge refuses fail-closed when channel observations cannot be restored;
  merge/unmerge history carries the observation state.
