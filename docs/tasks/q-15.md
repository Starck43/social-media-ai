and repository lifecycle rules. Include root guidance, canonical subsystem
contracts, phase plans, active task cards, skill documentation and generated

only when it is broken or non-portable, retaining its meaning and date. Do not
silently rewrite past test results, product decisions or source observations.

are in scope.

drift and its code/source evidence, exact fixes, a complete internal-link check,
applicable documentation lint/checks, and a clean diff. Record unverified or
owner-dependent claims as open rather than guessing.
