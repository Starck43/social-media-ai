## Granular Sub-Steps Inside This Task

1. **`T03.1` — Permission & ACL Retrieval Service (`app/services/knowledge/retrieval.py`):** Filter indexed snippets from mounted `Source(DRIVE_FOLDER)`, email, and local gateway folders by `acl_scope` and `data_boundary` (`local_only | company | cloud`) before returning results.
2. **`T03.2` — Versioned Document Service (`app/services/knowledge/documents.py`):** Track document revisions by `content_hash` (`sha256:...`). Any edit to `body_text` creates a new revision and resets `status = "draft_invalidated"`, `approved_by = None` (`edits invalidate approval`).
3. **`T03.3` — Wire Draft Templates & Flip `ready=True`:** Connect `app/web/knowledge.py` -> `web/knowledge.html` and `app/web/documents.py` -> `web/documents.html`, then set `ready=True` for `knowledge` and `documents` in `app/web/nav.py`.

## Jinja2 Template Context Contract

- `app/web/templates/web/knowledge.html` (already in repo) expects `knowledge_snippets: list[dict]` with keys: `title`, `source_name`, `acl_scope`, `data_boundary`, `citation_anchor`, `revision_hash`, `synced_at`, `excerpt`, `extraction_limit_note`.
- `app/web/templates/web/documents.html` (already in repo) expects `documents: list[dict]` with keys: `id`, `title`, `version`, `content_hash`, `updated_at`, `counterparty`, `status`, `approved_by`, `unsupported_terms_note`, `body_text`.
