## Scope and acceptance

Create the `/app/cases` workspace tracking configurable business cases from initial request through supplier comparison, quotation/order/invoice distinction, and remote approval decision:
- Own all arithmetic, joins, currency/tax conversions, pack-unit normalization, and domain rounding in deterministic server services using `Decimal` (never LLM arithmetic).
- Preserve formula versions, editable assumptions, quote freshness, and explicit missing-price markers (`missing price` is never interpolated).
- Support remote decision recording on a specific document version/hash while keeping unsupported terms visible, locking approved/signed content as immutable, and clearly distinguishing a product approval click from a legally recognized signature.

Accept with deterministic `Decimal` calculation tests, missing-price/currency-mismatch fixtures, and immutable approval-version tests.
