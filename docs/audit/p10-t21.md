Accepted task archive; earlier READY/pending notes below are historical.

Registry: [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md#p10); current status/owner live there.

## Scope and acceptance

Owner-requested follow-ups after the P8-T30 reverse-FK inlines and the table
registry landed:

1. `collected_items` must not appear as a “Собранный контент” section on
   `/admin/tenant/edit/1`. It is the largest table and its rows are long texts and
   media, so the section duplicated the collection pages and made the form heavy.
   Only that table is excluded; every other reverse-FK section keeps working.
2. The registry suites must stop depending on the literal table count: P10-T04
   added `login_attempts` (51 → 52 tables) and broke
   `test_registry_page_size_selection_is_bounded`, which is an obsolete
   expectation, not a registry defect.
3. Registry page styles live in `app/static/css/custom.css` (owner edit).

Accept: `INLINE_EXCLUDED_TABLES == {"collected_items"}` is honoured by
`related_inlines` for every console reader including the platform owner, a
regular child of the same workspace still renders, the registry page-size test
derives the count from `Base.metadata` and asserts the default-page behaviour,
and `docs/ADMIN.md` documents the deny-list.

## Out of scope

Digest-confirmation suites (P10-T20), stale `tests/ai` and `tests/security`
expectations (parallel agent), migrations, deployment acceptance.
