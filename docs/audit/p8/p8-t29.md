## Delivered behavior

One versioned task plan is stored and executed through shared services and
web/API/CLI/admin/agent surfaces. Plans express selected/discovered/all scope,
VK/TG platform filters, bounded queries and source ids, fixed or rolling windows
with timezone, once or scheduled triggers and an explicit collection budget.
One-off launches use a stable request key, fresh actor/membership/source rights,
an existing personal payer and quota admission, and a durable launch receipt.
Schedules and external sends retain their confirmation gates. Results expose
bounded, permission-filtered progress and outcomes. The P8-T37 correction carries
discovered source ids into analysis and shares mutation rules across surfaces.
The accepted chat-first lifecycle, composed digest runs and end-to-end journey
remain governed by P8-T39/P8-T40/P8-T41; this audit accepts only the shared
foundation and its own VK gate.
