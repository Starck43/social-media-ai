## What was pinned

1. **Integration before DONE.** `state=MERGED`, non-null `mergedAt` and merge
   commit, target `dev`, accepted head, then `git merge-base --is-ancestor` of
   the merge commit against a freshly fetched `origin/dev`, then `git ls-tree`
   of every delivered path. A rejected merge command, a queued merge and a
   closed-unmerged PR are explicitly not integration, and closing a PR is never
   a substitute for merging it.
2. **Alembic baseline before branch DB checks.** Compare the head derived from
   fresh `origin/dev` against the resolved shared `test_schema.alembic_version`
   and the candidate's graph. An unexplained mismatch is a
   baseline/integration failure, not a branch defect: stop bootstrap and
   reconcile rather than stamping past it.
3. **DB scenarios are mandatory.** Green unit/mock tests cannot replace a
   failing or unrun DB scenario, and deferring a required check does not permit
   READY.

The three originals the executor declared — `git diff --check` on the accepted
head and a cross-document wording consistency pass — hold on the merged text.

## Note on the squash ancestry

The PR was squash-merged, so the accepted head `fce83c68` is not an ancestor of
`dev` and no remote ref retains it. This audit therefore proves integration by
the reviewed squash-equivalence route the new rule itself allows — merge commit
ancestry plus per-path content comparison — rather than by head ancestry. That
is the documented exception, not a fallback around it.

## Limitation

The rule is enforcement by instruction, not by tooling: nothing in CI fails a
closeout that violates it. Recorded as a known limit; adding a checker would be
a separate task with its own scope.
