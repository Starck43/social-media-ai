# P20-T11 — Team work through connected task systems

Registry: [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md#p20); status, owner and dialogue live there. Parent: [P20](../phases/p20-proactive-assistant.md). Design: [WORKSPACE_UX](../WORKSPACE_UX.md). Request and owner decision: https://app.notion.com/chat?t=40fa7457748980d094a100a930619250

## Goal and dependencies

Let teams keep collaborating in services they already use instead of a second tracker here. Depends on the shared source-object registry introduced by [P16-T04](../audit/p16-t04.md) and on the [P13-T04 command ledger](../audit/p13-t04-p14-t04-business-crm.md) for writes; follows D24/D25/D26 ([Q-21](../audit/q-21-source-authority.md)).

## Scope

- Owner decision O-4: users choose their task service; do not mandate a single service for everyone. The owner selected Bitrix24 as the first connector for this task. Read only assigned and watched items from the current user's authorized portal connection, with a link, status and due date. Facts are read live and never mirrored locally; an unavailable source stays visible. Other services remain unsupported until their connectors ship.
- Show these items in «Задачи» and «Сегодня» as read-time entries; overlap with planner items is a suggestion, never an auto-merge.
- Writes (comment, status, assignee) are separate future capabilities through the command ledger with preview and approval; read and write consent are per connection. This increment implements read consent only and provides no write operation.

## Acceptance and checks

- Tests with mocked provider: paging/watermarks, rate limits, revoked token, cross-tenant isolation, conflicting edits (If-Match or provider equivalent), unknown write outcome, no mirrored status table.
- Verify service selection reflects available connectors and never implies unsupported services are connected. For Bitrix24, cover bounded paging, portal rate limits, revoked/insufficient task access, cross-tenant isolation and that write operations are unavailable without mutating the provider.

## Open doubts

Bitrix24 is the first delivered connector by owner choice. Users retain per-connection selection and read consent. The OAuth application must have Bitrix24 task-read permission; existing CRM scopes do not imply it. Team display does not broaden the provider identity or reveal another user's portal tasks. Read-only first; writes and additional providers remain future work.
