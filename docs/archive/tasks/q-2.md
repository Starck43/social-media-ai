### Why the media URLs are empty

Local reproduction (no network, no DB write) of the current normalizer with a
synthetic `wall.get` item carrying two photo sizes and a video preview returns
`[{'type': 'image', 'url': <largest size>}, {'type': 'video', 'url': None}]`,
plus a permalink, and drops `copy_history`. So:

- video previews are placeholdered **by design** (`_normalize_direct_attachments`
  never uses a video image as the media URL);
- photo snapshots lose their URL only when the incoming item had no accepted
  `sizes[]` entry or when it was staged before that code existed. The staged rows
  cannot tell which of the two happened, and both remain open;
- `app/utils/content_attachments.py` keeps `{'url': None}` deliberately
  ("missing media is not called empty"), which is why the audit saw 61 image and
  33 video placeholders rather than absent fields.

### Answer

- **Origin is certified for the audited batch:** all 51 posts are native wall
  posts of their own owners; none is a repost. The provider does return
  `copy_history` for reposts (3 posts seen among newer pages), so the audit's
  "origin not certified" statement was a limit of our stored data, not of VK.
- **Media references are not missing at the provider:** 61/61 photo attachments
  across the audited posts carry `sizes[]`. The stored `{"url": null}` snapshots
  therefore come from our collector/staging, not from VK. Registered as
  [P8-T52](../../tasks/p8-t52.md).
- Video previews stay placeholdered by design, and the provider video counts
  match the stored placeholders with no discrepancy.
- The DB holds 50 of the 51 audited rows; `-51955758_28874` was pruned since
  2026-10-18 (retention), which is why that row is verified by id instead.
