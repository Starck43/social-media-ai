### Resolver handoff examples

- Selecting no connection when two mailboxes are eligible yields `select_resource`. Each explicit selection keeps its own connection ID and folder.
- A disabled connection or revoked root yields `resource_unavailable`. A revoked personal grant yields `reauth_required`.

- Requesting a write against the Bitrix24 task-list capability yields `read_only`.
