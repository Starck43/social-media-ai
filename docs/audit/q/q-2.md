## Answer

- **Origin certified.** All 51 posts are native wall posts of their own owners:
  30 × `19149862`, 20 × `-55674807`, 1 × `-51955758`. None is a repost
  (`copy_history` absent) and none carries a foreign byline. VK does return
  `copy_history` for reposts — three such posts appeared among the newer pages —
  so the earlier "origin not certified" was our storage limit, not a provider
  limit: the normalizer never reads `copy_history`.
- **Media references exist at the provider.** 61/61 photo attachments on the
  audited posts carry `photo.sizes[]`; the stored `{"url": null}` snapshots are a
  collector/staging loss on our side. The video counts match the stored
  placeholders exactly (33), and video previews are placeholdered by design.
- **Stored batch is faithful.** 30/30 and 20/20 stored ids in the audited window
  were found at the provider with identical attachment-type composition. One
  audited row (`-51955758_28874`) is no longer in the database; it was verified
  by id instead (native post, one photo with sizes).
- **Permalinks** were never lost information: every row's link is derivable as
  `https://vk.com/wall{owner_id}_{post_id}`, and newer rows store it.
