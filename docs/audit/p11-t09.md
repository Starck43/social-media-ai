## Delivered contract

- Tool registration requires a canonical catalog permission; missing, unknown,
  public-sentinel and duplicate registrations fail. Names, schemas and handler
  signatures are checked before a tool enters the immutable public registry.
- Typed adapters validate inputs before handler invocation and validate results
  and JSON budgets. Nested payloads remain subject to their existing use-case
  validators; this does not claim field-level DTOs for broad dictionary results.
- Direct and confirmed dispatch share permission checks, and fresh actor rights
  are checked on every execution. Existing one-use confirmation remains bound
  to arguments, actor and tenant. Provider wire serialization stays at the
  provider boundary; logs retain only redacted metadata.
- Review found that a frozen `Tool` still exposed a mutable `ToolContract`, so
  callers could replace adapters or validation methods without changing the
  confirmation fingerprint. `ToolContract` and its adapter references are now
  frozen/read-only at the instance level, with mutation checks in the focused
  suite.
- The root-conftest route had a false-positive lazy-import assertion when
  `app.core.config` was preloaded. The test now forces that import cold within
  its patched module table and verifies the prior module is restored afterward.
