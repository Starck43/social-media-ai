# P20-T03 — Unified approval inbox on existing ledgers

Registry: [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md#p20); status, owner and dialogue live there. Parent: [P20](../phases/p20-proactive-assistant.md). Request: https://app.notion.com/chat?t=40fa7457748980d094a100a930619250

## Goal and dependencies

Provide one permission-filtered review surface for pending actions without creating a second approval authority. Initial scope is commands supported by the [P13-T04 command ledger](../audit/p13-t04-p14-t04-business-crm.md); add outbox and document projections only after [P15-T05](p15-t05.md) and [P19-T04](p19-t04.md) respectively exist and their owners' contracts are honored. Later projections follow the same rule when their owners land: calendar conflict and deletion approvals ([accepted P16-T03 contract](../audit/p16-t03.md), D4), 1C writes ([P17-T04](p17-t04.md)) and connected task-system writes ([P20-T11](p20-t11.md)). Preview/run for social tasks remains on its accepted plan path, not falsely represented as a ledger command.

## Scope

- List only actionable, authorized records with type, requester, source, target, exact content/version or safe preview, expiry, reason for review and current status. Link to the authoritative record; distinguish pending, rejected, expired, executed, failed and unknown outcomes. Redact data the reviewer may not see.
- Allow review of one exact item through its existing approve/reject action. Preserve actor, tenant, payload/hash, target, TTL, rights, consent, capability, single-use binding and execution-time rechecks. A review click is not an unconditional send or a legal signature; approval and separately admitted execution stay distinct where the owning contract requires it.
- A persistent `always allow` policy is **not authorized** by this card. Investigate as a separately reviewed, revocable, narrowly scoped proposal only where consistent with accepted per-item gates; external messages and other operations requiring explicit item approval keep that approval. No bulk approval or automatic execution in the pilot.
- One projection feeds every surface: the «На согласовании» list, the «Сегодня» section ([P20-T07](../audit/p20-t07.md)), the header badge and the chat «Ждут решения» tab and decision cards ([P20-T08](../audit/p20-t08.md)). Bot messages link to the web decision; in-bot approval buttons are a separate later task.
- Use the existing notifications surface and audit references; do not duplicate ledgers, user notifications or transport. Respect the outbox's sender-confirmation semantics (D14) and document revision invalidation.

## Acceptance and checks

- Tests cover cross-tenant and unauthorized viewers, inaccessible targets, stale/replaced payloads, expired and revoked approvals, concurrent/repeated clicks, rejected/unknown execution, CSRF, accessibility and separate execution. No operation bypasses its owning service. Mock external writes.
- Record exact focused commands and tested revision; include route/reference and isolated DB evidence only if the selected implementation actually requires them.

## Open doubts

A reusable approval policy changes the accepted approval contract and customer-visible behavior. Default: no persistent allow; if a use case needs it, present scope, revocation, risk and rollback to the owner before implementation. It is not approved by this planning card.
